# Returns policy

Orders can be returned within 30 days of delivery if the item is unused and in original packaging.
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days of warehouse receipt.
Final-sale items, opened software licences, and gift cards cannot be returned.
Customers in the Western Cape can drop returns at the Cape Town hub on Portswood Road.
A prepaid return label is emailed after the return is approved in the account portal.
